# AVAI Copy And SEO Skill

Use this skill for copywriting, messaging and search/social metadata.

## Brand Voice

- Language: Spanish, preferably neutral Latin American Spanish.
- Tone: technical, precise, credible and business-oriented.
- Avoid generic agency claims, inflated buzzwords and vague promises.
- Favor measurable outcomes, operational efficiency, automation, traceability and ROI.

## Messaging Rules

- Lead with the operational problem and business impact.
- Explain technical solutions in plain terms without losing precision.
- Use CTAs that imply a concrete next step: audit, diagnosis, consultation or review.
- Keep claims credible. If a number is used, make sure it fits the surrounding case or section.
- Avoid duplicating service cards or repeating the same benefit with different labels.

## SEO Checklist

- Each page should have a unique title and description.
- Titles should include AVAI when useful, but not at the cost of clarity.
- Descriptions should be concise and conversion-focused.
- Add canonical and Open Graph metadata when editing layout-level SEO.
- Preserve `lang="es"` and Spanish content conventions.

## Preferred Terms

- eficiencia operativa
- automatizacion de procesos
- software a medida
- dashboards ejecutivos
- arquitectura de datos
- trazabilidad
- diagnostico tecnico
- consultoria tecnica
